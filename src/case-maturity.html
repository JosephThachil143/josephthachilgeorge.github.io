What a maturity model is

A maturity model describes a practice in levels, from first steps to well established. Teams place themselves on the scale, read what the next level looks like and decide what to improve. It is a team exercise for self-improvement, not a top-down audit.

How domains at TUI benefit

  • One shared yardstick Every team in a domain talks about security in the same terms, so a conversation about security starts from a common scale.
  • A clear next step Instead of a long checklist, a team sees the next level and knows what to work on first.
  • Progress over time Repeating the assessment twice a year turns security into a habit and makes progress visible.
  • Ownership stays with the team Teams assess themselves, so the team owns the result and the improvement.

Enabling Teams & Learning Paths

Role-specific security learning paths and hands-on enablement that help everyone in the domain think and act with a security mindset in their daily work.

The request

The domain owner asked for security enablement for the benefit of the whole domain. I led the work from scratch, released it and improved it with feedback from team leads, heads of technology, architects and engineers.

Five paths, one per role

  • Head of Technology (HOT) — Strategic Governance, risk acceptance, quality and SLA outcomes.
  • Technology Team Lead (TTL) — Tactical Enabling the team and secure delivery.
  • Architect — Design Threat modelling, secure-by-design and patterns.
  • Information Security Manager (ISM) — Operational Compliance, policy, risk and incidents.
  • Engineer — Hands-on Secure coding, tooling and remediation.

How the paths work

  • Essentials first Each path has a mandatory core of about 5 hours, plus optional add-ons only where they add value. Total 5 to 8 hours, self-paced.
  • Free resources first TryHackMe (free rooms), PortSwigger and TUI's existing security standards. O'Reilly is a supplement.
  • Apply it Every path ends with how to use the learning in day-to-day work.

How the domain benefits

Around 800 people in the domain benefit. In their daily work they can think and act like a security person and keep a security-minded approach.

My role

I led the learning paths from scratch, on the domain owner's request. Alongside them I led 13 threat-modelling sessions, trained 8+ teams and ran 9 hands-on labs, and presented at HoLAC, Security Town Hall, ETF and Security Champions.

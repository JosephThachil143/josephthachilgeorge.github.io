MII — Machine Identity Intelligence

An open-source platform I built to make machine identities — IAM roles, OIDC federations and CI/CD tokens — visible, measurable and fixable: discovering them across AWS and GitLab/GitHub, mapping their trust chains, scoring their risk, simulating blast radius, and generating exact remediation.

Why I built it

Machine identities — IAM roles, OIDC federations, CI/CD tokens, service accounts — now outnumber human identities by as much as 82:1 in cloud environments, and they are the #1 attack vector for cloud breaches. Yet most organisations have no centralised way to see, audit or risk-score them. Existing tools list permissions, but they don't map trust chains, simulate attacks, or track how risk accumulates over time. I built MII to close that growing blind spot.

You can't secure what you can't see — MII makes machine identities visible, measurable and fixable.

The problem it solves

  • Invisible access You can't see which pipelines or identities can reach which cloud accounts — or what each can actually do once it has access.
  • Overprivilege goes unnoticed Overprivileged roles — often full admin on CI/CD — accumulate silently, and trust granted at group or org level expands as projects are added.
  • Hidden attack surface Missing branch restrictions, wildcard trust and unused-but-active roles pile up as attack surface nobody is tracking.
  • Unknown blast radius If a pipeline is compromised, nobody can quickly tell how far the damage could spread.

How it works

Discovery Connects to AWS (read-only) and auto-discovers every IAM role, trust policy, permission and CI/CD identity.
Trust graph Builds a directed graph of who-can-assume-whom, visualising hidden lateral-movement paths.
Risk scoring (0–100) Scores every identity across six weighted factors: admin, production access, trust count, cross-account, staleness and unused.
Findings & compliance Flags dangerous patterns and runs 8 automated policy checks with pass/fail evidence.
Blast-path simulation "If this identity is compromised, what's reachable?" — traced step by step through the trust graph.
Trust Debt (a metric I designed) Quantifies accumulated unnecessary trust, graded A–F — like tech debt, but for permissions.
AI remediation Role-aware AI classifies each identity type and produces exact CLI commands and Terraform to fix it.
Reports & export Exportable PDF/Excel reports give auditors concrete pass/fail evidence.

Why it matters for large organisations

  • Scale — AWS Organizations auto-discovery scans 100+ accounts from one config, with no per-account manual effort.
  • Prioritisation — security teams get a ranked list of the most dangerous identities instead of raw permission dumps.
  • Audit-ready — compliance checks and exportable reports give auditors concrete pass/fail evidence.
  • Reduced breach impact — blast-path simulation shows exactly where to cut off lateral movement before an attacker exploits it.
  • Continuous governance — Trust Debt scoring lets leadership track whether the environment is getting safer or riskier over sprint cycles.
  • Faster remediation — engineers get copy-paste fixes, cutting the time from "finding" to "fixed".
  • No vendor lock-in — open source, self-hosted, works across AWS + GitLab/GitHub, and supports any OpenAI-compatible AI provider, including free local models.
Bottom line: MII turns an invisible, manual, one-role-at-a-time audit problem into continuous, automated, organisation-wide visibility — reducing both breach risk and audit effort.
